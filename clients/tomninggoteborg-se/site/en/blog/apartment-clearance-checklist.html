Apartment clearance checklist

A well-prepared clearance is faster, cheaper and smoother. Use our checklist so you don't miss anything important.

Whether it's a move, an estate or a rental to be emptied, a clear plan helps enormously.

2–3 weeks before

  • Book the date and request a quote.
  • Cancel or move electricity, broadband and home insurance.
  • Register your change of address.
  • Decide what to keep, donate or remove.

The week before

  • Clearly mark what to keep.
  • Gather valuables, keys and documents.
  • Empty and clean the fridge/freezer.
  • Book any lift or loading zone with the association.

On the day

  • Leave keys and clear instructions – you don't have to be present the whole time.
  • Make sure there's parking for the truck.
  • Review the result with us before handover.

After the checklist

What happens on clearance day

On the day itself the work follows a set order. We start by confirming what is marked to be kept, then work room by room – usually beginning furthest from the door so nothing has to be moved twice. Items are sorted as we go into keep, donate, sell, recycle and waste, rather than being piled up and dealt with later.

Most normal-sized flats are finished within a day. If the flat is being handed back to a landlord, a move-out cleaning finishes the job so it is ready for inspection.
