Estate clearance in Gothenburg – a complete guide for next of kin

Clearing an estate raises many questions at an already difficult time. Here we walk through what actually needs to be done, and in what order.

When a relative passes away there is usually a home to be cleared – often while the family is grieving and handling the estate inventory.

1. Start with the estate inventory

Before clearing begins, the estate inventory (bouppteckning) should be done or at least started, so nothing of value is mishandled. Valuables and documents should be set aside first.

2. Sort into three: keep, donate, recycle

Dividing belongings into keep, donate and recycle from the start makes the whole job faster. You decide where the lines go.

3. What's included

A professional estate clearance includes a free, discreet assessment, careful packing and removal, eco-friendly recycling (around 98%), and rough cleaning so the home is ready for handover or sale.

4. What does it cost?

Price is driven by volume, access and how much cleaning is required. We give a fixed price after assessment.
