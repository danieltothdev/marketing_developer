How pricing works

What affects the price of a clearance?

We always give a fixed price after a free home visit or a photo-based assessment – you know exactly what you pay before work begins. The price is mainly driven by four factors:

1

Volume

The amount of belongings, measured in cubic metres or number of transports, is the biggest factor.

2

Access

Floors without a lift, narrow stairwells and distance to the truck affect working time.

3

Sorting & recycling

Reusable items are donated – hazardous waste and mixed refuse carry tip fees.

4

Cleaning

Clearance only, or also rough or move-out cleaning before sale and handover.

RUT deduction

When does the RUT deduction apply?

The Swedish RUT deduction may cover the labour cost of certain household services, such as removal and cleaning connected to a household. It normally does not cover tip fees or pure business and BRF assignments. We always state clearly in the quote which part is RUT-eligible.
