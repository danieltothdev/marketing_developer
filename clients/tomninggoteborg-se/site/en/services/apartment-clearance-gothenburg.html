Overview

What is professional apartment clearance?

Apartment clearance requires more than carrying out furniture. Stairwells must be protected, lifts booked and heavy lifts planned. Our team knows Gothenburg buildings from studio flats to large apartments.

Whether for move, estate, eviction or sale you get a fixed quote after inspection – sorting, junk removal and optional cleaning included in the full solution.
