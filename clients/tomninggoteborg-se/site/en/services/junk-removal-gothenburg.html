Overview

What is professional junk removal?

Professional junk removal means bulky waste is handled safely from your door to approved reception. We carry, load, sort and document – you avoid hiring a van, heavy lifting and navigating tip rules.

Our Transport & Recycling Hub in Gothenburg ensures materials are classified for maximum recycling and reuse. Working furniture can go to charity instead of landfill – saving both environment and money.
