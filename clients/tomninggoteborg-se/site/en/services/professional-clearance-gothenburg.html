Overview

What is professional house clearance?

Clearing a home, office or storage unit is a major project. Many people underestimate the physical effort and time it takes to sort, carry and transport furniture and belongings. Professional clearance means an experienced team takes over the entire job.

Clearance is more than throwing items into black bags. We work methodically to separate what can be donated, recycled or must be disposed of. With local expertise in Gothenburg we understand logistics in narrow stairwells in Linné and large villa gardens in Askim – and offer a seamless chain from start to finish through estate clearance, housing association projects and junk removal.

Process

How clearance works – step by step

We believe in a transparent, structured process so you can feel confident everything is handled with environmental care and respect for the property.

1

Initial consultation & inspection

We start with a call to understand your needs. We often offer a free home visit or ask for photos to assess volume and conditions in Gothenburg.

2

Planning & timeline

After inspection we present a fixed quote and clear schedule. Local expertise helps us navigate city logistics smoothly.

3

Sorting & categorisation

On site we categorise items: valuables for reuse, recyclables (wood, metal, electronics) and general waste.

4

Removal & transport

Heavy furniture is dismantled to protect door frames and stairwells. Everything is loaded into our vehicles for safe transport.

5

Eco-friendly recycling

All material is weighed in and delivered to certified recycling centres. Nothing is wasted unnecessarily – core to our environmental commitment.

6

Final cleaning & check

When spaces are empty we carry out basic floor cleaning, or full move-out cleaning if agreed.

7

Documentation & follow-up

We hand over an empty, clean property and can provide receipts and recycling certificates for businesses and housing associations.
