Overview

What is bulk waste and who can use our service?

Bulk waste includes furniture, mattresses, appliances and large cardboard – items that do not fit regular bins. We collect from doors, courtyards or association premises without you or the board doing heavy lifting.

For housing associations we offer planned clean-up days and emergency collection when recycling rooms overflow. For residents it is a simple alternative to van hire – fixed price and eco-friendly final handling included.
